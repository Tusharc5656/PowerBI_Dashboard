# Project Details & Methodology

## 📊 Dataset Overview
- **Total Records:** 5M+ disaster records
- **Time Period:** 2018–2024
- **Geographic Coverage:** 190+ countries
- **Economic Data:** $250M+ in losses
- **Disaster Events:** 7,000+ yearly occurrences

---

## 🔍 Analysis Methodology

### 1. Data Exploration
- Analyzed disaster data structure and quality
- Identified key variables: casualties, economic loss, disaster type, country, date
- Assessed data completeness and anomalies

### 2. Quantitative Analysis
- Conducted trend analysis across 7 years
- Performed seasonal pattern identification
- Analyzed regional correlations
- Calculated casualty rates and economic impact metrics

### 3. Advanced Metrics Created
- **By Country:** Total casualties, economic loss, disaster count
- **By Disaster Type:** Percentage distribution, average impact
- **By Year:** Year-over-year trends, growth rates
- **KPIs:** Total casualties, total economic loss, average loss per disaster

---

## 💡 Key Insights Discovered

1. **Geographic Hotspots:** Identified countries with highest disaster frequency and casualties
2. **Disaster Type Impact:** Each disaster type contributes 9–10% of total casualties
3. **Temporal Trends:** Seasonal patterns in disaster occurrences
4. **Economic Concentration:** Top countries account for >$10B USD in losses
5. **Correlation Patterns:** Relationships between casualty rates and economic loss
